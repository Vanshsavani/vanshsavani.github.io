Privacy Policy

Die-Cast Idle Factory · Effective 19 October 2026

Die-Cast Idle Factory is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: we, the developer, do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve them, as described under Advertising below.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and none of your game data is sent to us.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress the game saves two small files in its own storage on your device:

  • your factory progress (cash, upgrade levels, licences, your daily streak and tutorial progress), and
  • your settings (sound volumes, and whether vibration and reminders are on).

These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them, and Settings > Reset progress inside the game erases your progress at any time.

Permissions

  • Vibration - for the short haptic feedback when you tap, which you can switch off in Settings.
  • Notifications - for optional reminders, described below. Android asks you before the game can show any.
  • Internet access - used only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID - used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads. You can reset it, or delete it, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game can leave up to two reminders on your device when you close it: one when your factory has stopped earning, and one when your Daily Shipment is ready. They are scheduled by your phone itself, contain nothing about you, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else. No reminder is shown between 10 pm and 8 am. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings (Reminders) or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one (for example to double your earnings while away, or for a Rush Order). It never shows ads you did not ask for.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device: your advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location), information about your device and this app, your interactions with the ads, and diagnostic information. This is collected and used by Google, not by us, under Google's own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first whether ads may be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.

Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.
